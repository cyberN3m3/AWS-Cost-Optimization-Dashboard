How the scan works

It only reads
The scanner has Describe, List and Get permissions and nothing else. It cannot stop, delete or change a resource.
There is no public endpoint
Scans run every Monday, or on demand with aws lambda invoke. This page never talks to AWS: it shows sample data, or a report file you open, and that file stays in your browser.
The prices are AWS's own
Spend and the forecast come from Cost Explorer, and EC2 prices from the Price List API. Anything priced from published us-east-1 rates is marked as an estimate.
