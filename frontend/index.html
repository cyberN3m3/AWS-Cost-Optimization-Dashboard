    How the patrol works

    It looks, it writes it down, it tells you. It never switches anything off on its own: every fix stays your decision.

    Walk

    Every Monday at 08:00 UTC it checks every region of your account, with read-only access.

    Write

    Each thing you pay for but don't use gets a ticket with its monthly cost, in plain words.

    Tell

    You get the tickets by email, biggest first, with what to do about each one.
